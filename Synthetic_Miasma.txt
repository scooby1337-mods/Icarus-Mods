Synthetic Miasma - Miasmic tools without a swamp
by scooby1337

On Olympus and Styx there is no swamp - and no Crystallised Miasma. This mod adds two recipes
to the Chemistry Bench, so you can craft miasmic tools, arrows, bolts and ammo on every map:

- Extract Venom: 3 Scorpion Tail -> 1 Venom Sac
- Synthetic Miasma: 3 Poison Paste + 2 Poison Sac + 1 Venom Sac -> 1 Crystallised Miasma

Ingredients:
- Scorpion Tail: desert and arctic scorpions
- Poison Paste: Mortar & Pestle (2 Spoiled Meat + 1 Sulfur)
- Poison Sac: Cave Worms, Sulfur Worms, Land Sharks

No existing recipes or items are changed. Works on dedicated servers (install on the server too).
